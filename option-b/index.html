I

What's included, room by room

Every standard visit follows the same list, so you know what to expect before we arrive. Sample list

Kitchen

  • Counters and backsplash wiped and sanitized
  • Sink and faucet
  • Outside of appliances
  • Cabinet fronts spot-cleaned
  • Trash emptied, floor vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Toilet, tub and shower cleaned and disinfected
  • Sink, counters and fixtures
  • Mirrors
  • Trash emptied, floors cleaned

Bedrooms

  • Reachable surfaces dusted
  • Beds made
  • Carpets vacuumed
  • Hard floors mopped

Living areas

  • Dusting
  • Vacuuming and mopping
  • Trash emptied
  • Tidying

A deep clean adds

Baseboards, blinds, doors and door frames, ceiling fans, air vents, light switches and window tracks, plus built-up grease, soap scum and grout.

Move-in or move-out adds

Inside cabinets and drawers, inside the oven, and inside the fridge and freezer. The home must be empty.

Usually not included

Hand-washing dishes and exterior areas such as porches and decks. Laundry and the inside of the oven are available as add-ons.
