FAQ

Quick answers.

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. Access is arranged with the office before the first visit.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes, the team brings its own products and equipment. If you'd like a specific product used, tell us.

What about pets?

Tell us about pets before the first visit. Keep pets that might slip out an open door secured. Pet accidents are not part of a standard clean.
