What a standard clean covers, room by room

A standard clean covers these four areas. Deep and move-in/out cleans add the extras below. Sample list

Kitchen

  • Counters and backsplash wiped and sanitized
  • Sink and faucet
  • Outside of appliances
  • Cabinet fronts spot-cleaned
  • Trash emptied
  • Floor vacuumed and mopped

Bathrooms

  • Toilet, tub and shower cleaned and disinfected
  • Sink, counters and fixtures cleaned and disinfected
  • Mirrors
  • Trash
  • Floors

Bedrooms

  • Reachable surfaces dusted
  • Beds made
  • Carpets vacuumed
  • Hard floors mopped

Living areas

  • Dusting
  • Vacuuming
  • Mopping

Questions people ask before the first visit

Do I need to be home?

Usually not. Access is arranged with the office before the first visit.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes, the team brings its own products and equipment. If you'd like a specific product used, tell us.

What about pets?

Tell us about pets before the first visit. Keep pets that might slip out an open door secured. Pet accidents are not part of a standard clean.
